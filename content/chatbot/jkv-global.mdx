# General FAQ

## What is forex?

Forex, or foreign exchange, is the global market where currencies are exchanged and traded in pairs.

Currencies are traded relative to one another, such as EUR/USD or GBP/USD.

## What is a CFD?

A CFD, or Contract for Difference, allows a trader to speculate on the price movement of a market without owning the underlying asset.

CFDs are leveraged products and involve significant risk.

## What is leverage?

Leverage allows a trader to control a larger position using a smaller amount of margin.

Leverage increases market exposure and can magnify both profits and losses.

## What is a spread?

The spread is the difference between the bid and ask price of a trading instrument.

Trading spreads vary according to the account, instrument and market conditions.

## What is margin?

Margin is the amount of capital required to open or maintain a leveraged position.

Margin requirements depend on the product, leverage and account conditions.

## What is KYC?

KYC means Know Your Customer.

It is an identity-verification process used during account registration and for applicable compliance requirements.

## What is a demo account?

A demo account allows users to explore a trading platform using simulated funds rather than real capital.

It can be used to become familiar with platform functionality before live trading.
